## Consequence for this paper

This blocks a contribution framed as a new planner-versus-learned-policy hierarchy or primitive selector for
contact-rich assembly. A pure E6 capability heatmap is therefore not enough for a systems paper. The only possible
E6 direction is a distinct, rigorously evaluated claim about evaluation-induced controller-decision error.
