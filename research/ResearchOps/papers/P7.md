## Relevance

Directly relevant to demonstration filtering and the question whether data quality can improve downstream policy learning at fixed budget.

## Limits

Smoothness alone may remove contact-recovery demonstrations. Our quality score must include task outcome, reference feasibility, constraint margin and diversity.
