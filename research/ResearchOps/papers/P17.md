## What is verified

- The framework combines DMP trajectory adaptation, hybrid trajectory/force learning, model-free RL for force-control
  parameters, and goal-conditioned/self-supervised imitation for motion drift and insertion bottleneck states.
- It aims to generalize a trajectory profile from one task instance to topologically similar variations, with
  simulation and real-hardware evaluation.
- The XML describes DMP coupling terms, trajectory/force modules, and the adaptive framework; it does not describe a
  fixed physically reproducible candidate pool or cross-round allocation of a common correction budget.
