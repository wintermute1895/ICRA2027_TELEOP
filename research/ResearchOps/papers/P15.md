- Year: 2026

- Source: OpenAlex candidate scan on 2026-08-12

## Why it matters

The title directly claims phase-scheduled policy learning, slow planning and fast correction for contact-rich
manipulation. It is a direct novelty threat to a phase-aligned reference/residual recovery method.

## Direct evidence

- arXiv HTML full text was inspected on 2026-08-12. PhaForce uses a contact-aware phase predictor that estimates
  contact probability and phase belief, a low-rate diffusion planner, and a high-rate phase-routed residual corrector.
- It uses visual and 6D force/torque observations. Its fast residual is routed to interpretable corrective subspaces.
- Its real-robot plug-in experiments discuss rim collision, partial insertion, slip-induced rotation, phase-gated
  recovery, retreat-and-retry, and OOD geometric shifts.
- It reports an average 86% success rate and +40 percentage-point gain over its listed baselines. These are the
  authors' reported results, not independently reproduced evidence.

## Consequence for this paper

- PhaForce closes the PAR branch: a phase-scheduled slow/fast contact correction method is not a defensible primary
  contribution for this project, particularly without F/T sensing.
- The paper may use contact/recovery phases as evaluation labels, but not advertise phase-aware residual control as its
  method contribution.
