## What is verified

- The official arXiv API abstract was retrieved on 2026-08-13.
- CRSAIL rolls out complete learner trajectories, then queries an expert at only selected visited states; it scores
  novelty through distance to labelled expert states and calibrates a query threshold with conformal prediction.
- The abstract explicitly distinguishes this post-rollout procedure from methods needing real-time expert takeover.

## Consequence for this paper

This blocks claims that RFC is the first offline/post-rollout state-query rule, coverage/novelty allocation method, or
no-real-time-takeover collection procedure. It does not, from the abstract alone, establish an LRI fixed physical
candidate pool, reference-relative coordinate, or target-weighted recovery-risk evaluation. RFC needs a matched
representation-novelty control when feasible and must otherwise limit its claim to its declared allocation family.
