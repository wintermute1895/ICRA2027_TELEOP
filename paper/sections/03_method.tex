\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

At time $t$, the correction model consumes a history of raw teleoperation
commands $u^{raw}$, measured robot state $q$, and frozen visual embeddings $z$:
\begin{equation}
  \hat{u}_{t+1} = f_\theta(u^{raw}_{t-H+1:t}, q_{t-H+1:t}, z_{t-H+1:t}).
\end{equation}
The deployed command is a bounded residual relative to the human command:
\begin{equation}
  u^{out}_t = \Pi_{safe}\left(u^{raw}_t + \alpha(\hat{u}_{t+1} - u^{raw}_t)\right).
\end{equation}

Human and visual-language annotations identify correction intervals for sample
selection and loss weighting. Future correction labels are never an online
input. The fixed safety gate and the operator retain final authority.
